This GitHub Pages site will provide your private, Google-authenticated view of your personal transactions. Your financial records will stay in your Google Sheet; they will not be stored in this repository.

  1. Enable GitHub Pages for this repository.
  2. Configure the Google OAuth client for this site's exact web origin.
  3. Connect the app to your Transactions sheet using Google sign-in.

Security: Do not add your Google OAuth client secret, access tokens, spreadsheet data, or passwords to this repository. A GitHub Pages site is publicly accessible; access to the data must be enforced by Google authentication.
